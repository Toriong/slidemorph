## Full Description

### 📸 SlideMorph - Insert Any Image Format in Google Slides

Google Slides has strict image format limitations, only accepting GIF, JPG, and PNG files under 50 MB and 25 megapixels. **SlideMorph** breaks these barriers by converting unsupported formats directly in your browser—no server uploads required!

### Supported Formats

✅ **HEIC & HEIF** - iPhone and modern camera photos  
✅ **PDF Pages** - Select and insert any page from a PDF document  
✅ **SVG** - Scalable vector graphics with perfect clarity  
✅ **WebP** - Modern web image format  
✅ **AVIF** - Next-generation image compression

### Key Features

🔒 **100% Client-Side Processing**  
All image conversion happens locally in your browser. Your files never leave your computer—no server uploads, no privacy concerns.

📄 **PDF Page Picker**  
Need just one page from a multi-page PDF? SlideMorph shows you thumbnail previews of every page so you can pick exactly the one you need.

⚡ **Automatic Optimization**  
Images are automatically downscaled to meet Google Slides' 25 MP and 50 MB limits while maintaining quality.

🎯 **Two Insert Modes**  
- Insert on the current slide at a default size
- Replace a selected image while maintaining position and size

🎨 **Clean, Modern Interface**  
Beautiful sidebar with drag-and-drop file selection, real-time status updates, and intuitive controls.

### How It Works

1. **Open SlideMorph** from the Add-ons menu in Google Slides
2. **Select your file** by clicking or dragging and dropping
3. **For PDFs:** Choose which page to insert from thumbnail previews
4. **Choose your action:** Insert new or replace existing image
5. **Click Process & Insert** - Done! Your image appears on the slide

### Privacy & Security

- ✅ Minimal permissions (presentations.currentonly only)
